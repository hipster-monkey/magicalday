i · who you are

They named you Karishma,
and the universe took notes.

Karishma. Magic, A wonder, A miracle that is made real. Out of all the names in all the languages, this was the only one the world could give you, because no other word would have been enough.

You are that calming silence before a song begins. You are the small, impossible joy of finding your favorite line in a book you've read. You are every good thing together at once.

ii · the things we do

A small scrapbook
of us.

Other people have ordinary lives. We have a museum of weird things, and most of them don't make any sense, and that's exactly why I love them.

✦ One

Uno, fairly, always

Late nights. The deck shuffled. You winning. Always you winning. Through some unexplainable phenomenon of the universe, never anything else. Definitely not cheating. Wink. Wink. One day I'll catch you in middle of it again, and again I'll just laugh and let you have it.

✦ Two

Conversations that make zero sense

Half our talks would not survive a transcript. 3 AM theories. Sentences that loop back into themselves. Whole debates about nothing, won by no one. And somehow, those are the ones I remember best.

✦ Three

Ghosts, djinn, and Korean men

Your three deepest research interests, in no particular order. One night you're convinced something is in the room. The next, you're explaining the entire backstory of a man with very good cheekbones and a tragic past. Both equally serious. Both equally compelling. I've learned to nod and never interrupt.

✦ Four

A sleeper who clings

You sleep hugging. Like the world might float away if you don't keep it close. It's the softest thing about you, and the most honest.

✦ Five

The list of wild things

Your fantasies. Your wishlists. The slightly chaotic list of dreams. We're going through them, one by one, in no particular order, with no end in sight. Take your time, love. I'm in no hurry. I have the rest of the list to get to.

✦ Six

Show ADHD, professionally

We start a series. Three episodes in, deeply invested. Then a trailer flashes, and suddenly we're starting another one. Our watchlist is a graveyard of half loved stories. We've watched the first episode of more shows than most people finish in a year.

✦ Seven

Pink magic in a parking lot

Near Raffa. Random cars. No witness. Pink magic. Some of the best moments of my life have happened in that exact parking lot. Not in any city. Not in any apartment. Right there.

✦ Eight

Subway sandwiches at Ibis One Central

Of all the restaurants in this city. Of all the places we could be. Sandwiches. At an Ibis Subway. And somehow, I wouldn't trade that hotel bences for any candlelit dinner in the world. love is a combo meal with no olives.

none of these would mean anything to anyone else. that's the whole point.

iv · wishes for your year

For the year ahead,
I wish you soft things.

First

More late night Uno. Even if you keep winning. Maybe especially if you keep winning.

Second

More conversations that don't make sense, but feel like the most important ones in the world.

Third

Every ghost you wish to meet, every djinn story that gives you goosebumps, and one Korean man with the cheekbones of your choosing.

Fourth

More nights of falling asleep tangled up, holding on like the world might float away, while I make sure it doesn't.

Fifth

Every wild thing on your list, ticked off, slowly, properly, with the kind of patience that makes the wanting half the fun.

Sixth

More pink magic, more parking lots, more Subway at the Ibis. The kind of small joys that are entirely, completely, secretly ours.

Seventh

A whole pile of unfinished shows. We'll finish them. (We probably won't.)

And, always

The certainty, wherever you go and however the year turns, that you are deeply, quietly, completely loved.

v · a quiet promise

Wherever this year takes you,
I'm here.

For the loud days and the slow ones. For the ones where everything makes sense, and the ones where nothing does. For the ones you'll want to remember, and the ones you'll need someone to remember with you.

Some people you meet, and some people you recognize. You were always the second kind.
